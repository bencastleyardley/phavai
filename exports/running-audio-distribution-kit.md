## Forum contribution outlines

Only use an outline when it directly answers an existing question. Write the answer in full on the platform before sharing a link.

### Someone asks OpenRun Pro 2 or OpenFit 2

1. Explain the format split: connected neckband versus independent ear hooks with a case.
2. State the normalized battery numbers: 12 hours versus 11 hours per charge and 48 hours after case recharges.
3. Mention that both carry IP55, so the decision is mostly format and routine.
4. Ask whether the runner uses hats, long hair, collars, or a hydration vest.
5. Link to the direct comparison only if links are allowed and useful.

### Someone asks whether open-ear is safer

1. Avoid a guarantee. Say that leaving the canal open can preserve more ambient sound.
2. Explain that volume, wind, route complexity, traffic, and attention still matter.
3. Contrast physical open-ear designs with microphone-based transparency.
4. Recommend a route-specific pause or low-volume rule.
5. Link to the format explainer only when it adds detail.

### Someone asks for a running-headphone shortlist

1. Ask whether they run outdoors, indoors, or both.
2. Separate awareness, secure fit, water resistance, and sound isolation.
3. Give two relevant options instead of pasting the entire ranking.
4. Share the specification database as a neutral comparison tool.
5. Disclose that Phavai may earn from qualifying purchases if sharing an affiliate-supported page.
