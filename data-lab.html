The contract

What a Phavai tool must show.

Inputs stay visible

Every output shows the assumptions that produced it, so you can change the decision instead of trusting a black-box answer.

Claims stay attached to sources

Manufacturer facts, independent tests, and owner observations are labeled separately rather than blended into one unsupported claim.

Uncertainty stays on the page

Conflicting measurements, older generations, and evidence gaps remain visible. The tools narrow research; they do not manufacture certainty.

How these resources are built

  1. AI assists with organizing source material, normalizing fields, and checking internal consistency.
  2. Ben Yardley reviews the published inputs, calculations, evidence labels, cautions, and links.
  3. Interactive results run locally in the browser. No account, upload, or personal profile is required.
  4. Retail links stay outside the tools so a commercial destination cannot change the result.
Read before using

These are decision aids, not hands-on product tests, medical guidance, or safety guarantees. Manufacturer specifications can change, personal fit varies, and real-world battery life depends on settings and conditions. Follow the linked source trail and verify the exact model before buying.
