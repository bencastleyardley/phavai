Buying questions

Best Laptop Stands for Remote Work FAQ

Do I need an external keyboard with a laptop stand?

Yes, for proper ergonomics. Raising the laptop screen while typing on the laptop keyboard usually creates a new wrist and shoulder problem.

Is a fixed or adjustable laptop stand better?

Fixed stands are simpler and sturdier. Adjustable stands are better when desk height, chair height, or user height varies.

Can a laptop stand replace a monitor?

Sometimes, but an external monitor is still better for all-day work. A stand is the cheaper, smaller first upgrade.
