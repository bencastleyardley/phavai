Method

How to use this database

  • AI assists with organizing and normalizing the fields; Ben Yardley checks every row against the linked manufacturer document.
  • Manufacturer product pages and technical specifications supply the normalized fields.
  • Single-charge runtime stays separate from case-assisted total runtime.
  • An ear canal that remains open is labeled separately from microphone-based transparency modes.
  • The database describes products. Independent expert and owner evidence determines Phavai's ranking.

Limits: This is a curated comparison of four guide finalists, not every running headphone on the market. These are manufacturer claims, not Phavai lab results. Battery varies with volume, calls, codecs, features, temperature, and device settings. Fit and sound are personal, and product availability can change.
