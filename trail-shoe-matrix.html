Method

How labels are assigned

  • A terrain label needs two independent exact-model sources, or one specialist expert source plus direct video or owner corroboration.
  • Manufacturer specifications establish facts; they do not establish real-world grip, comfort, fit, or durability by themselves.
  • Fit labels require agreement across at least two sources. Disagreement becomes fit-sensitive or unverified.
  • Available in wide is not treated as equivalent to an anatomical toe box, and distance use is not inferred from stack height alone.
  • Conflicting measurements stay separate instead of being averaged.

Limits: Phavai did not hands-on test these shoes. Fit is personal, terrain changes with weather, and this matrix synthesizes selected public evidence rather than every review online. Use it to make a try-on shortlist, then confirm the exact model, current generation, sizing, return terms, and local trail demands.
