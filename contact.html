Correction checklist

  • Link the Phavai page and name the exact product or passage.
  • Describe what appears incorrect, incomplete, or out of date.
  • Include a direct source URL rather than a search-results page when possible.
  • Explain why the source is authoritative or what first-hand context it adds.
  • Include the relevant model, version, market, and date for recalls, redesigns, or specification changes.

What happens next

Phavai prioritizes safety issues, materially wrong specifications, mismatched products, broken evidence, and changes that could alter a recommendation. Submitted claims are checked before publication; a link alone does not guarantee a change.

We may update the guide, add context, replace a source, or leave the current wording in place if the available evidence does not support a correction. Every message is useful, but response times vary and we cannot promise an individual reply.
