Transparent math

What the planner calculates

Buffered race timeExpected elapsed time × (1 + contingency %)
Usable hours after reservePublished mode hours × (starting charge % − finish reserve %)
Buffered headroomUsable hours after reserve − buffered race time
Minimum starting chargeFinish reserve % + buffered race time ÷ published mode hours

Limits: Manufacturer figures are maxima, not guarantees. Firmware, satellites, temperature, display use, sensors, navigation, music, and battery age can shorten runtime. The toggles flag those risks but do not apply invented drain percentages. Fully charge the watch, rehearse the exact settings on a long training effort, and keep a verified charging contingency when navigation or safety depends on it.
