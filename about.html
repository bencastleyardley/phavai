What Phavai does

Phavai uses AI-assisted research to organize a curated set of expert reviews, product-specific videos, owner discussions, and official product details before you start comparing tabs. It surfaces recurring praise, complaints, and disagreements; a human editor checks the evidence and owns the final recommendation.

Phavai guides are evidence syntheses, not claims that we personally tested every product. If Phavai ever performs original hands-on testing or measurements, the guide will say exactly what was tested, by whom, and how.

The goal is not to make every decision automatic. The goal is to remove enough noise that a shopper can make a confident call without feeling pushed.

How we are different

  • The top pick is clear, but the caveats are not buried.
  • The best reviews sit beside the recommendation.
  • Retail links do not affect ranking order.
  • Every guide explains who should skip the product.
  • We avoid vague social claims unless they can be checked cleanly.

Human accountability

Ben Yardley

Founder and human editor of Phavai

Ben's professional background includes Outside Media, Trail Runner Magazine, and UltraSignup. He has run the UTMB 100-mile race and has been climbing since age 15.

For running and outdoor guides, Ben checks source-to-product matches, challenges conflicting claims, applies practical trail and endurance context, and approves the final recommendation. AI helps organize the research; Ben remains accountable for what gets published.

Scope matters: Ben's outdoor background supports Phavai's running and outdoor coverage. Broader consumer guides are human-reviewed, but Phavai does not present that outdoor experience as category expertise in unrelated products.

Built for the last ten minutes before purchase.

Phavai pages are designed around the questions shoppers actually ask: what should I buy, why did it win, is it right for me, what could go wrong, and which sources should I trust?

Best reviews first

Recommendations begin with useful reviews and owner feedback, not link availability.

Category-specific

A trusted trail-shoe source is not automatically trusted for headphones, desks, or coffee makers.

Proof nearby

The winner, tradeoffs, pros, owner complaints, and source links appear directly on buying-guide pages.

Trust infrastructure

Trust is the product.

Phavai is designed so source quality, editorial judgment, and retail links stay separate. That makes the site easier to improve without quietly compromising the recommendation.
